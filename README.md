# Pizza-Sales-Insights-Revenue-Trends-Customer-Behavior
This personal data analytics project analyzes pizza sales data to uncover revenue trends, customer ordering behavior, product performance, peak sales periods, and potential growth opportunities. Using SQL and Power BI, I transformed raw sales data into actionable insights that can support better pricing, menu decisions, and revenue strategies.

Key Insights

. Revenue growth is driven primarily by increased order volume rather than higher customer spending.

. Customers are placing smaller orders, creating an opportunity to increase basket size.

. Thursday to Saturday records the strongest demand.

. Tuesday is the weakest-performing day.

. Large pizzas are the strongest revenue contributors.

. Specialty pizzas have strong customer preference.

. XL and XXL pizzas contribute relatively little revenue.

. Lunch and dinner are critical revenue windows.

. May shows a noticeable decline, suggesting possible seasonality.

. The menu has a relatively balanced category mix.

Recommendations

. Introduce combo deals and meal bundles to increase average order value.

. Capitalize on Thursday–Saturday demand with weekend promotions and group offers.

. Introduce targeted Tuesday promotions to improve low-day performance.

. Promote bestselling pizzas such as Thai Chicken Pizza.

. Position large pizzas as the best-value option.

. Reassess the pricing and positioning of XL and XXL pizzas.

. Use lunch and dinner promotions to maximize peak-hour revenue.

. Plan seasonal campaigns ahead of periods such as the May decline.

. Guide customers toward high-value and high-margin menu options.
